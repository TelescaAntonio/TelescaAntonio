I design and build autonomous AI systems on hardware most people would consider inadequate — or impossible. My work spans AI agents, cybersecurity and systems engineering, with one constant: **powerful AI on consumer hardware, not enterprise budgets.**

## 🔬 Selected work

- **[t2-egpu-nvidia](https://github.com/TelescaAntonio/t2-egpu-nvidia)** — Solved a problem open since 2019: modern NVIDIA eGPUs on Apple T2 Intel Macs under Linux. Characterized two undocumented T2 kill mechanisms and developed a software-only fix (Resizable BAR windowing). Validated: RTX 3090 running a 27B-parameter LLM at 36 tok/s, fully from VRAM, on an "incompatible" MacBook Pro.
  📄 Preprint: [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22011607](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22011607)
- **HashSigil** — Live cryptographic certification platform: proof of existence/integrity/authorship via triple-hashing, ECDSA, Bitcoin timestamping (OpenTimestamps) and BNB Smart Chain attestation. eIDAS-relevant PDF certificates. → [hashsigil.eu](https://hashsigil.eu)
- **LiveInk-AI** — First platform keeping published articles factually up to date automatically (MC-TDC method, −85% token usage). Serverless on Cloudflare Workers, 15+ data sources, in production. → [liveink-ai.com](https://liveink-ai.com) · SIAE Reg. D000030735
- **SENTINEL-AI** — Multi-agent AI intrusion detection system for NIS2 compliance of European SMEs, running on a Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W. 📄 [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.18819290](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18819290) · SIAE Reg. D000030683

## 💡 Philosophy

Enterprise results don't require enterprise infrastructure. A used consumer GPU, a $200 NAS, a rooted phone — engineered properly, they outperform their price class by an order of magnitude. Every project I publish is open source, documented for reproducibility, and runs on hardware anyone can buy.

> *"The best AI is the one that runs on what you already have."*
